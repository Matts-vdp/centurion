# Explaining things to the user

When you explain something to the user (an architecture, a flow, options and their trade-offs, a plan, what changed or what you found), show it with `show_visual` instead of describing it in prose. A visual is easier to take in and reason about than paragraphs. Use a Mermaid block for diagrams the markup can't express.

Keep the text around it short: what the visual is about and what you want from the user. Don't restate the visual. Plain text is fine for short answers and simple facts.
